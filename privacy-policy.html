Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2026

GlyphGuard is an offline-first mobile app that helps people inspect suspicious links, domains, QR content, text, and files before opening them. Core character and text analysis runs locally on the device. When a user asks for online threat intelligence or reputation checks, the app may send limited request data to GlyphGuard services and, when the final AI second-opinion stage is enabled, to an AI service provider selected by GlyphGuard so the check can complete.

Local by default

Core text, character, and most history/settings processing stays on-device.

Online only when needed

Some URL, domain, reputation, abuse-protection, and optional AI second-opinion stages use GlyphGuard network services or service providers.

No ad tracking

GlyphGuard does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics inside security checks.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the GlyphGuard mobile application and related threat-checking services operated by Voldirex ("GlyphGuard", "we", "our", or "us").

Contact: glyphguard.app@gmail.com

2. Data We Process

We try to minimize what leaves the device. However, GlyphGuard is not a zero-network app when a user runs online threat checks, downloads threat updates, triggers abuse-protection reporting, reaches the optional AI second-opinion stage, or triggers the IPQS fallback after an uncertain AI result.
Category Examples Where it is processed
Local content Text you paste, files you open, QR payloads, local history, settings Mainly on your device
Online check requests Domain or URL submitted for a reputation or threat lookup, plus redacted URL metadata and selected explanation language tag used for AI second-opinion review Sent to GlyphGuard threat services and, when needed, to service providers such as Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal, the configured AI provider, or IPQS acting on our behalf
App-scoped identifier A random app-generated device identifier stored by the app Sent with online lookup and abuse-protection requests
Request context Input source such as manual, QR, clipboard, file, or incoming share Sent with online lookup and threat-report requests
Security telemetry Abuse or repeated over-limit attempt signals, threat reports for confirmed dangerous URLs Sent to GlyphGuard security endpoints when needed to protect the service
Network metadata Standard request metadata such as IP address and timing seen by hosting or security infrastructure Processed as part of delivering and securing network services

3. How We Use Data

  • To perform local text, character, file, and QR analysis on the device.
  • To run online threat intelligence, domain reputation, and URL safety checks when requested by the app flow.
  • To ask a configured AI provider for a final second opinion on borderline URL or domain cases.
  • To download and refresh threat data used by the app.
  • To enforce quotas, detect bypass attempts, investigate abuse, and keep the lookup service reliable.
  • To accept confirmed dangerous URL reports that help improve future protections.
  • To answer support or privacy requests sent to us by email.

4. What Stays on Your Device

GlyphGuard stores certain data locally so the app can work and remember your preferences. This may include:

  • App settings, language, and visual preferences.
  • Local analysis history and saved results on eligible plans.
  • A random app-scoped identifier used for service protection and quota enforcement.
  • Locally cached threat data and locally cached dangerous verdicts.

You can remove local app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

5. When Data Leaves the Device

Data may leave the device in the following situations:

  • You run an online URL, domain, or reputation check.
  • The app reaches the optional AI second-opinion stage for a borderline online check.
  • The app refreshes required threat snapshots or manifests.
  • The app sends abuse-prevention or over-limit telemetry.
  • The app reports a confirmed dangerous URL or domain to GlyphGuard services.

In these cases, the app sends only the information needed to complete the security action. GlyphGuard does not use ad SDKs and does not sell personal data.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

We may use hosting, storage, security, infrastructure, and AI service providers to run GlyphGuard services. Those providers may process limited data on our behalf to deliver, secure, and maintain the service.

We may also disclose information if required by law or if reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, or security harm.

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the app and we do not sell personal data.

7. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall the app.

Server-side lookup, abuse-prevention, and security records are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, investigate abuse, maintain reliability, and meet legal obligations.

8. Your Choices

  • You can avoid optional online checks by not using flows that require them.
  • You can clear local history or app data through app and device settings.
  • You can uninstall the app to stop further local storage and app-based processing.
  • You can email us about privacy questions or requests at glyphguard.app@gmail.com.

9. Children

GlyphGuard is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information directly, contact us and we will review the request.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the services we operate. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to limit data exposure and to process as much as possible on-device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date on this page.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: glyphguard.app@gmail.com
